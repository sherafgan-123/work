# Competitive analysis: B2B outbound and growth sites

Research date: 2026-10-25. Method: each homepage was fetched and its text content read. "Verified" means it appeared in the fetched page text. "Interpretation" is my own reading. Visual design (colour, type, motion, loading speed, accessibility) could not be verified from text, so I make no claims about it. Operatix, RevPartners and Martal could not be read (empty page or HTTP 403) and were replaced.

## Ten patterns that shaped this site

1. **One primary CTA, repeated.** Refine Labs and CIENCE use a single named action ("Book a strategy call"). This site uses "Book a Strategy Call" everywhere.
2. **Name the audience.** Refine Labs states who it is for. The hero here names AI, SaaS and IT companies.
3. **Metrics with definitions.** Refine Labs footnotes its windows; Predictable Revenue shows numbers with no context. Every figure here is defined and never mixed across types.
4. **Case cards lead with one number.** Belkins, CIENCE and SalesRoads put a headline metric on each card. The featured cases here do the same, with different layouts.
5. **Filterable case directory.** SalesRoads filters by service. This site filters by industry.
6. **Say the commercial terms plainly.** CIENCE and SalesRoads state contract terms. Services here avoid prices and guarantees and say so.
7. **Qualifying form fields.** Leadium asks timeline and budget. This form stays short (five fields) because no pricing exists to qualify against.
8. **A point of view.** Predictable Revenue's manifesto and Sopro's free tools show authority. The "research discipline" statement and the Research, Relevance, Revenue frame do this here.
9. **Consistency builds trust.** Sopro and Leadium show conflicting figures on one page, which weakens credibility. Every number here is stored once in data files.
10. **A human voice and a reply expectation.** Growth Society's personal tone and response promise stand out. Copy here is first person, with no promised response time because none was provided.

## What was deliberately not copied

No layouts, wording, imagery or branding were taken from these sites. Interpretation: the category is crowded with logo walls, stat counters and "book a demo" buttons. This site differs by being a named individual specialist, using an editorial serif voice, a light palette and honest, defined metrics.
